MULTI-AGENT ARCHITECTURE

Many specialists. One answer.

A team of specialized intelligent operators coordinated around a central orchestrator to understand, retrieve, clarify, and synthesize grounded responses.

⚡ ORCHESTRATOR Query Flow
🧭
Query Understanding
Classifies intent, normalizes input, and filters non-retrieval conversational chit-chat.

Detects ambiguous pronouns or underspecified queries and proposes options.
🧠

✍️
Response Generation
Grounds context into final response with strict source citations and truth checks.
KNOWLEDGE GAP DETECTION

It should know when it doesn't.

A true enterprise intelligence platform must refuse to invent facts. When evidence does not exist in your database, AI-QRS transparently reports a knowledge gap rather than hallucinating.

⚠️ ZERO EVIDENCE DETECTED

"What is the employee maternity leave policy of Infosys?"

Because no document named "Infosys Policy" exists in the database, the model enforces strict grounding:

"I could not find an answer to your question in the available knowledge base."
🛡️

Hallucination Guard Active

Threshold: Cosine similarity < 0.20 rejects speculation. Answers require verifiable chunk citations.

ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 2026

1. WELCOME
Welcome to ACME Corporation. This handbook outlines the policies, expectations, and benefits that apply to all employees. Please read it carefully and retain it for future reference.

2. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM local time. Employees are entitled to a 30-minute unpaid lunch break and two 15-minute paid rest breaks per day. Flexible working arrangements may be available upon manager approval.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE
All full-time employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis. Leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance and approved by the line manager. Unused leave may be carried over to the following year up to a maximum of 10 days.

4. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required. Sick leave beyond 10 days will be processed under the Company Medical Benefits Policy.

5. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Maternity leave entitlement is 16 weeks fully paid. Paternity leave entitlement is 2 weeks fully paid. Both entitlements are available after 6 months of continuous employment.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, a replacement day off will be granted within 30 days.

7. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCEDURE
Step 1: Log into the Employee Self-Service portal at portal.acme.internal.
Step 2: Select "Leave Management" from the main menu.
Step 3: Choose the type of leave (Annual, Sick, Maternity, Paternity, or Other).
Step 4: Enter the start and end dates.
Step 5: Attach supporting documents if required.
Step 6: Submit for manager approval.
Step 7: You will receive an email confirmation once approved or declined.

8. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: in June and December. The review covers goal achievement, competency assessment, and development planning. Ratings range from 1 (Does Not Meet Expectations) to 5 (Exceptional Performance).

9. CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism, integrity, and respect. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must not disclose confidential company information to any external party without written authorisation. This obligation continues for 12 months after employment ends.

11. IT AND EQUIPMENT USAGE
Company equipment including laptops, mobile phones, and software licences are for business use only. Personal use is permitted on a minimal and incidental basis. Employees must not install unauthorised software.

12. DRESS CODE
Business casual attire is expected on all working days. Smart business attire is required for client-facing meetings.

13. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
Employees with a workplace grievance should first discuss the matter with their line manager. If unresolved, the matter may be escalated to Human Resources. Formal grievances must be submitted in writing within 30 days of the incident.

14. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Employees wishing to resign must provide a minimum of 4 weeks written notice. The company reserves the right to terminate employment with cause or with 4 weeks notice in accordance with applicable employment law.

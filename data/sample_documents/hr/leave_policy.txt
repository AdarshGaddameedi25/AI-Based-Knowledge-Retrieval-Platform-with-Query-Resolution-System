ACME CORPORATION — LEAVE POLICY
Document Reference: HR-POL-002 | Version 1.4 | Reviewed: March 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the leave entitlements, procedures, and responsibilities for all ACME Corporation employees across all employment categories.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all permanent full-time and part-time employees. Contract employees and interns are covered under separate agreements.

3. LEAVE TYPES AND ENTITLEMENTS

3.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees: 21 working days per calendar year.
Part-time employees: Pro-rata based on contracted hours.
Leave Year: January 1 to December 31.
Maximum Carry-Over: 10 days to the next leave year.
Leave Encashment: Not permitted except upon resignation or termination.

3.2 Medical Leave
Entitlement: 10 working days per year for outpatient sick leave.
Hospitalisation Leave: Up to 60 days per year (inclusive of outpatient sick leave).
Certification Required: For absences of 3 or more consecutive working days.

3.3 Maternity Leave
Duration: 16 consecutive weeks.
Paid: Fully paid for employees with at least 6 months of service.
Unpaid Extension: Up to 4 additional weeks may be requested.

3.4 Paternity Leave
Duration: 2 weeks within 16 weeks of the child's birth.
Paid: Fully paid for employees with at least 6 months of service.

3.5 Compassionate Leave
Duration: Up to 5 working days for the death of an immediate family member.
Immediate family is defined as: spouse, child, parent, or sibling.

3.6 Study Leave
Available for approved professional development courses: up to 5 days per year.
Must be approved by HR and the relevant department head at least 21 days in advance.

3.7 Unpaid Leave
May be granted at management discretion for personal reasons.
Maximum duration: 30 calendar days per year.

4. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS

Step 1: Employee submits leave request via the Employee Self-Service portal (portal.acme.internal).
Step 2: Line manager receives email notification and reviews the request within 2 working days.
Step 3: If approved, leave is deducted from the employee's entitlement balance.
Step 4: If rejected, the manager must provide a written reason.
Step 5: For medical leave, medical certificate must be submitted within 3 working days of returning to work.

5. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
Managers must ensure adequate staffing coverage before approving leave requests.
Managers must not approve leave that would reduce team capacity below 50 percent.
Managers should review and respond to leave requests within 2 working days.

6. LEAVE DURING NOTICE PERIOD
Annual leave during the notice period must be mutually agreed upon.
The company reserves the right to reject annual leave applications during the notice period.

7. FORFEITURE
Leave not taken and not carried over will be forfeited at the end of the leave year.
Medical leave cannot be encashed or carried over.

SECTION 1: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
=======================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes guidelines for reimbursement of business-related 
expenses incurred by employees in the performance of their duties. All 
employees who incur expenses on behalf of the organization are subject to 
this policy.

1.2 ELIGIBLE EXPENSES

The following categories of expenses are eligible for reimbursement:

TRAVEL EXPENSES
- Airfare: Economy class for domestic flights; business class for 
  international flights exceeding 6 hours
- Hotel: Up to $250/night for standard cities; up to $350/night for 
  high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo)
- Ground transportation: Taxis, rideshares, rental cars at standard rates
- Meals: Up to $75/day (breakfast $15, lunch $20, dinner $40)
- Parking: Actual cost with receipt

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
- Conference registration fees: Pre-approved by department head
- Professional certifications: Up to $2,000 per year per employee
- Books and technical subscriptions: Up to $500 per year

OFFICE AND EQUIPMENT
- Home office supplies: Up to $150 per quarter for remote employees
- Software licenses: Approved by IT department
- Equipment repairs: Pre-approved by facilities management

1.3 INELIGIBLE EXPENSES

The following expenses will NOT be reimbursed:
- Personal entertainment (movies, sporting events, personal travel)
- Alcoholic beverages (except during approved client entertainment)
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or other violations
- First-class airfare upgrades (unless approved medical necessity)
- Spouse or family member travel expenses

1.4 SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

All expense reimbursement requests must:
- Be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense
- Include original receipts for all expenses over $25
- Use the company's approved expense management system (ExpenseCloud)
- Be approved by the employee's direct supervisor
- Include business purpose and attendees for client entertainment

1.5 APPROVAL THRESHOLDS

- Up to $500: Direct supervisor approval
- $500 to $2,000: Department head approval
- $2,000 to $10,000: Finance department approval
- Over $10,000: CFO approval required

SECTION 2: BUDGET MANAGEMENT
=======================================================================

2.1 ANNUAL BUDGET CYCLE

The annual budget cycle begins on October 1st each year:
- October: Department budget requests submitted
- November: Finance review and consolidation
- December: Executive approval and finalization
- January 1: New fiscal year budget takes effect

2.2 BUDGET CATEGORIES

Operating Budget: Day-to-day operational expenses including salaries,
  supplies, software, and utilities.

Capital Budget: Long-term investments in equipment, technology 
  infrastructure, and facility improvements (assets over $5,000 
  with useful life over 1 year).

Project Budget: Discrete project funding with defined start/end dates 
  and deliverables.

2.3 BUDGET VARIANCES

Budget variances exceeding 10% of allocated amounts require:
- Written explanation of variance cause
- Revised forecast for remainder of period
- Action plan to return to budget (if overspent)
- Department head sign-off

Favorable variances over 15% must also be explained to ensure 
accurate future forecasting.

2.4 BUDGET TRANSFERS

Budget transfers between line items within the same department:
- Up to $1,000: Manager discretion
- $1,000 to $5,000: Department head approval
- Over $5,000: Finance director approval

Cross-departmental transfers require Finance director approval 
regardless of amount.

SECTION 3: PROCUREMENT AND PURCHASING
=======================================================================

3.1 COMPETITIVE BIDDING REQUIREMENTS

All purchases must follow competitive bidding requirements:
- Under $2,500: Single quote acceptable
- $2,500 to $10,000: Minimum two written quotes required
- $10,000 to $50,000: Minimum three written quotes required
- Over $50,000: Formal RFP (Request for Proposal) process required

3.2 PREFERRED VENDORS

The Finance department maintains a list of approved preferred vendors. 
Purchasing from preferred vendors:
- Eliminates need for competitive bidding under $10,000
- Ensures contract and compliance pre-verification
- Provides pre-negotiated pricing

To add a vendor to the preferred list, submit a Vendor Registration 
Form to the Finance department with:
- Business registration documents
- Insurance certificates
- Tax identification number
- Banking information for payment setup

3.3 PURCHASE ORDERS

All purchases over $1,000 require a Purchase Order (PO) before 
commitment. Purchase Orders must include:
- Vendor name and contact information
- Detailed description of goods/services
- Unit prices and total amount
- Delivery terms and expected delivery date
- Budget account code

3.4 PAYMENT TERMS

Standard payment terms are Net 30 (payment due within 30 days of 
invoice receipt). Early payment discounts should be taken when 
economically beneficial (e.g., 2/10 Net 30 means 2% discount if 
paid within 10 days).

Late payments may result in vendor penalties and are tracked by 
the Finance department for vendor performance reviews.

SECTION 4: FINANCIAL REPORTING
=======================================================================

4.1 INTERNAL REPORTING CALENDAR

Monthly Reports (due by 5th business day of following month):
- Department profit/loss statement
- Budget vs. actual variance report
- Headcount and labor cost report

Quarterly Reports (due by 15th business day of quarter-end month):
- Quarterly financial statements
- Cash flow projection
- Capital expenditure tracker

Annual Reports (due by March 31st):
- Audited financial statements
- Annual budget vs. actual comparison
- Three-year financial projection

4.2 KEY FINANCIAL METRICS

Finance tracks the following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- Revenue growth rate (target: 15% YoY)
- Operating margin (target: 20% or higher)
- Operating expense ratio (target: below 65% of revenue)
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO): target under 45 days
- Current ratio (target: above 2.0)
- Debt-to-equity ratio (target: below 0.5)

4.3 FINANCIAL CONTROLS

To maintain financial integrity:
- Segregation of duties: No single employee controls authorization,
  execution, and recording of financial transactions
- Dual signature requirement for payments over $10,000
- Monthly bank reconciliations
- Quarterly internal audits by Finance team
- Annual external audit by independent certified public accountants
- Access controls: System access limited to job function requirements

SECTION 5: CORPORATE CREDIT CARDS
=======================================================================

5.1 ELIGIBILITY

Corporate credit cards are issued to:
- Department heads and above (automatic eligibility)
- Frequent travelers (4+ trips per year)
- Employees with recurring business purchase needs (upon manager request)

5.2 CREDIT LIMITS

Standard card limits:
- Individual contributor: $2,000 monthly limit
- Manager: $5,000 monthly limit
- Director: $10,000 monthly limit
- VP and above: $25,000 monthly limit

5.3 ACCEPTABLE USE

Corporate cards must ONLY be used for:
- Business travel and accommodation
- Client entertainment (with prior approval)
- Office supplies and equipment
- Software and subscriptions
- Conference and training fees

5.4 PROHIBITED USE

Corporate cards must NOT be used for:
- Personal purchases of any kind
- Cash advances
- Gift cards (without Finance pre-approval)
- Gambling or adult entertainment
- Political contributions

5.5 REPORTING AND RECONCILIATION

Cardholders must:
- Submit monthly expense reports within 5 days of statement close
- Attach receipts for all transactions over $25
- Provide business purpose for all transactions
- Report lost or stolen cards immediately to Finance and card issuer

Failure to submit timely reports may result in card suspension.
